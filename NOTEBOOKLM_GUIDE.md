# 🎧 How to Create Your "Video" in NotebookLM

Your **Training Video Generator** creates a perfect `.docx` file optimized for Google's NotebookLM. Here is how to turn that file into an amazing Audio Overview (podcast-style training).

## 1. Export Your Tutorial
1. In the **Training Video Generator** app, make sure you have your steps, narration, and screenshots captured.
2. Click the **"Export for NotebookLM"** button in the top right.
3. Save the `.docx` file (e.g., `MyApp-User-Guide.docx`).

## 2. Import into NotebookLM
1. Go to **[NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google.com/)** and sign in.
2. Click **"New Notebook"**.
3. Give your notebook a title (e.g., "MyApp Training").
4. In the **"Add sources"** panel, click **"Google Docs"** or **"Upload source"** (depending on where you saved it, but "Upload source" -> "File" is usually best if you downloaded it locally).
   - *Select the `.docx` file you just downloaded.*

## 3. Generate the Audio Overview
1. Once the source is uploaded, look for the **"Audio Overview"** section (usually on the right side or top of the dashboard).
2. Click the **"Generate"** button.
   - *Note: It may take 3-5 minutes to generate.*
3. **Wait for the magic!** 🪄

## 4. The Result
You will get a "Deep Dive" audio conversation where two AI hosts discuss your tutorial!

- They will **walk through the steps** you defined.
- They will **read out** (or paraphrase) your AI-generated narration.
- They will discuss the **screenshots** (since they are embedded in the doc).
- It sounds like a professional podcast or radio show discussing your software!

## 💡 Troubleshooting
- **If the audio is too short:** Add more details to your step titles and narration in the Generator app.
- **If they miss a step:** Ensure the step has clear headers (which our exporter does automatically).
- **Want to customize?** You can type instructions in the NotebookLM chat box like "Focus more on the login process" before generating, although the Audio Overview is mostly automatic based on the source.
